The Millionaire Mindset
  • Hum Salesman nahi, Business Consultants hain.
  • Hamara kaam "Bechna" nahi, "Select" karna hai.
  • Confidence is 90%, Content is 10%.
  • Goal: Har call se ek Relationship ya ek Selection nikalna hai.

The Perfect Greeting (The First 30 Seconds)

  • Energy Check: Aapki awaaz mein smile honi chahiye.
  • Script: "Hi [Name], main [Your Name] baat kar raha hoon [Location] se. Maine aapka enquiry form dekha tha..."
  • Permission: "Kya ye sahi waqt hai 2 minute baat karne ka?"
  • Rule: Naam ka istemal 2-3 baar karein (Personal Touch).

Need Analysis - The 'X-Ray' Phase

Bina Problem Jane, Solution Mat Do!

1. Family: Background samjho.
2. Occupation: Job/Studies se kitne khush hain?
3. Recreation: Khali samay mein kya karte hain?
4. Money: Kitna extra kamana chahte hain?
Killer Questions:
- "Aapko extra income kyu chahiye?"
- "Agar ye kaam nahi mila, toh aapka Plan B kya hai?"

Filter - Serious vs. Not Serious

Case A: Serious Hai?

-> Move to Plan Pitch.

Case B: Serious Nahi Hai?

-> Give a Reality Check.

  • "Mehangai (Inflation) 10% se badh rahi hai, kya aapki salary badh rahi hai?"
  • "Degree se zyada Skills ki value hai."
  • "Career Growth = Digital Business + Network Marketing."
